# FlyOver

Everybody has probably used **Flightradar24** to find out what plane just left that jetstream above their head, where it came from, and where it’s going.

As an aviation geek, I find myself opening the app multiple times a day. But hunting around on a busy world map every time gets old fast.

**FlyOver** turns your desk into a personal, real-time sky spotter.

Instead of browsing a global map, it puts **your location at the center**. By picking up the radio signals planes broadcast directly out of the sky with a small USB antenna, it tells you what is passing above you with zero delay.

---

### What it does

* **Tells you where to look:** Shows the compass direction and how high to tilt your head so you can spot the plane out the window.
* **Predicts overhead passes:** Gives you a countdown before a flight passes directly above you.
* **Shows the details:** Pulls the airline, plane model, and route for whatever is in sight.

---

### The Plan

* **Step 1: The Terminal (CLI)** — A clean, fast dashboard in the terminal that tracks what’s overhead right now.
* **Step 2: The E-Ink Display** — A small gadget for the desk or windowsill that quietly shows traffic passing by and alerts you when something interesting flies over.
